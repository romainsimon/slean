# SL-009: decision protocol for optional 3D

**Status: prototype prepared, study not run.** Slean has a validated local 2D Explorer and accessible relation list in [PR #3](https://github.com/romainsimon/slean/pull/3), plus a synthetic ET/OU gate case in stacked [PR #4](https://github.com/romainsimon/slean/pull/4). A disposable local [2D/3D comparison prototype](https://github.com/romainsimon/slean/commit/9d575f21d435ab1c10e000ee612a19ffb10d3e50) was prepared on a separate branch from the gate candidate later included in PR #4. No representative ET/OU dossier or target-reviewer comparison exists. This protocol defines the evidence required before shipping a Z-axis view; it does not authorize a scientific campaign or private-data publication.

The prototype has also been ported onto the integrated candidate stacked above PR #17, so the two study conditions use the same current Explorer code and checked bundle. Its local manifest stays `prepared_not_run` and records SHA-256 identities for the agent-projected bundle and both condition pages, without a raw owner-input digest. A clean source revision is required. This is preparation for a fair comparison, not participant evidence or a decision to ship 3D.

The approved PRD requires a with/without-Z dependency-reading task on representative dossiers and a recorded result. The synthetic 0.3 case verifies local map behavior but cannot substitute for that input. The disposable prototype uses one checked, agent-projected timeline and the same selected prefix, decision, evidence list, exact event and source bundle in both conditions. Only the visual map differs. Its manifest says `prepared_not_run`; it is not wired into the product Explorer. Once authorized representative dossiers and reviewers are available, freeze their inputs and both conditions before testing. Keep their artifacts and task results outside the publishable synthetic fixtures unless publication is separately authorized.

At `9d575f2`, the clean local build passed 16 tests. Browser checks found identical embedded bundles, prefix, decision, evidence text, exact event and map record IDs in both conditions. The 3D controls changed the projection by keyboard and reset to their defaults; the map updated at prefixes 11, 12 and 13. At 390 and 320 px, the page had no horizontal overflow outside the map. With reduced motion enabled, the study controls and map had no timed transition or animation. The Impeccable detector reported one non-blocking line-length warning. These are mechanical checks, not participant outcomes.

## Inputs and participants

- Obtain three authorized, representative dossiers that a research reviewer would actually inspect: a chronological decision with cost, converging prerequisites (ET), and alternative paths (OU). Keep their source and visibility policy; use the same audience-filtered export in both UI conditions. If no such dossiers can be shared for this task, do not substitute the single synthetic threshold example and claim a representative result.
- Freeze a 2D baseline and a 3D candidate at named commits. Both must expose the same records, selected prefix, text table, cost and unknown state. The 3D candidate may add viewpoint controls but no extra data or precomputed answer.
- Recruit six people who review experiments or agent runs. Assign three to 2D-first and three to 3D-first; use matched dossiers in opposite order to limit learning from one answer. Record the participants' roles and prior familiarity without collecting private research content in the result sheet.

## Tasks and measurements

Give each participant a selected journal prefix and three questions, without showing the answer: (1) identify the observations and protocol that support the decision at that prefix; (2) report the recorded cost and its coverage, distinguishing missing cost from zero; (3) for ET/OU, state which dependencies are all required and which are alternatives. After each answer, ask for the cited record IDs. Use the same wording in both conditions and allow the normal text table in both.

For every participant, condition, dossier and task, record completion (correct/incorrect/abandoned), wrong or omitted record IDs, elapsed time from task reveal to final answer, hints or resets, and whether the participant used the table. Record keyboard-only and reduced-motion checks separately. Keep raw task results, including null and negative outcomes; publish only an authorized aggregate.

## Decision rule

Use the dependency task as the primary comparison. The proposed local decision rule is to consider shipping 3D only if at least four of six participants are no less accurate in 3D, the median correct-answer time is at least 20% lower in 3D, and no task has more wrong attributions or abandoned answers. These are provisional product thresholds for a small usability study, not PRD numbers or statistical proof; freeze them before the first participant sees a candidate. A preference poll or attractive screenshot alone does not pass.

Regardless of speed, a 3D candidate must keep the equivalent text table and selected-prefix state available, remain keyboard operable, make viewpoint motion interruptible, and respect reduced motion. If any requirement fails, or the measured benefit is unclear, keep the 2D Explorer and record the result as no demonstrated 3D benefit. No animation may alter events, costs, observations or decisions.
